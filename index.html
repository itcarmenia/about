About

A living industry
Armenia's textile tradition spans millennia of weaving, silk-work and rug-making. In the Soviet era, Gyumri (then Leninakan) grew into the region's "city of masters", home to large-scale spinning and weaving combines. Today's manufacturers build on that base with modern equipment and export-grade certification. Armenian factories already produce for well-known houses including Moncler, Prada, Max Mara etc., exporting under the "Made in Armenia" label.

This directory is compiled by International Trade Center / ITC for international sourcing outreach. Specs (MOQ, lead time, capacity, export markets) are as reported by each company; contact them directly to confirm current terms.
